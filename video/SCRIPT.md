# Confam demo video: script (storyline A)

Narrator: Nigerian male voice (YarnGPT2). About 400 words, about 3 minutes. Every claim on screen comes
from the live app, the repo or the README's cited sources.

| # | Beat | On screen (real footage unless noted) | Narration |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Problem | Title card: "Debited. Not credited." over a phone frame | You buy airtime. Your account is debited. The airtime never lands. Then you spend two weeks chasing a refund. |
| 2 | Problem | Card: 200.3 million mobile lines in Nigeria (NCC, Sep 2026); 1.63 million Woyofal meters in Senegal | Every day, millions of us top up phones and prepaid meters. When the money moves first and the product comes later, the risk is all on us. |
| 3 | Solution | Live app home, EN, country Nigeria | This is Confam. E no land? You no pay. You pay for airtime, data and electricity with Bitcoin, and your payment only completes after the provider confirms delivery. |
| 4 | Setup | Tap Connect, Get a free test wallet, balance appears | Connect a wallet. For this demo, a free test wallet with twenty-five thousand test sats. |
| 5 | Happy path | Airtime form: Use test number, MTN, ₦200, Continue | Pick airtime. Enter the number, the network, the amount. Confam shows the exact price in sats. |
| 6 | Mechanism | Order page: lock clamps, "Payment locked", wallet balance drops | Now watch. My sats are locked, not sent. They are held in the Lightning network by a hold invoice. Confam cannot take them yet. |
| 7 | Mechanism | Timeline: Buying it for you, Provider confirmed delivery, Payment completed; CONFAM stamp | Confam buys the airtime. Only when the provider confirms delivery does the payment complete. |
| 8 | Proof | Signed receipt card: "Signature valid" | And I get a receipt signed with Nostr, checked right here in my browser. |
| 9 | Climax | New order to a number that fails; lock opens; NOT CHARGED stamp; balance back | Now the bad day. This purchase fails. The lock opens by itself. Not charged. My sats never left my wallet. No refund to chase, no support ticket. |
| 10 | Senegal | Switch to FR, Sénégal, Électricité, Woyofal meter, token box | And in Dakar, the same guarantee for Woyofal electricity, in French, in CFA, with the token on screen. |
| 11 | Impact | Countries list: airtime in 19 countries, data in 13, electricity in Nigeria and Senegal | Airtime in nineteen African countries, data in thirteen, electricity in Nigeria and Senegal. |
| 12 | Impact | /api/stats in the browser: deliveryRate, medianSecondsToDelivery, chargedWithoutDelivery 0 | We measure it in public. Delivery rate. Time to delivery. And the number that matters most: charged without delivery. Zero. |
| 13 | Next | Card: real-money launch, TV and water bills, auto top-up, agents | Next: real money on our own Lightning node, TV and water bills, automatic meter top-ups, and agents who accept sats on the street. |
| 14 | Close | End card: Confam, live URL, GitHub | Confam. E no land? You no pay. |
