# Confam demo video: storylines

Requirement (criteria, "Clear Pitch & Docs"): a 3-minute demo, in the order problem → solution →
impact → next steps. Real footage of the live app (https://confam-qxdh.onrender.com), a Nigerian male
narrator (YarnGPT2), visible clicks with sound, a music bed.

## A. "E no land? You no pay." (chosen)
Opens on the pain every Nigerian knows: the debit alert arrives, the airtime or token never does, and
the refund takes weeks. Then the same purchase on Confam, paid with Bitcoin: sats lock, the provider
delivers, only then does the payment complete. The climax is the failure case: a purchase that does not
go through ends on **NOT CHARGED**, and the wallet balance is back where it started. Then Dakar: Woyofal
electricity, a real token screen. Close on the live metrics (`chargedWithoutDelivery: 0`) and what ships next.
*Why it wins:* the hook is lived, the mechanism is shown rather than explained, the failure case is the proof.

## B. "Two cities, one guarantee"
Lagos and Dakar side by side from the first frame: airtime in naira, Woyofal in CFA, French and English.
*Why not:* it splits attention before the viewer knows what the guarantee is.

## C. "Proof first"
Opens on `/api/stats` and the signed receipt, then walks backwards to the user.
*Why not:* starts with an API response; judges meet the person last.
