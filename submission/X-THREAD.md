# Confam: X thread (post after the demo video is up)

1/
Debited. Not credited.
Every Nigerian knows that SMS. You pay for airtime or a light token, the money goes, the product never comes.

We built Confam for #DakarBitcoinHack: pay bills with Bitcoin, and if it doesn't arrive, you were never charged. ⚡
E no land? You no pay.

2/
The trick is a Lightning hold invoice.
Your sats get LOCKED in the Lightning network, not sent.
Confam buys the airtime. Only when the provider confirms delivery does the payment complete.
No delivery in 90 s? The lock opens by itself. Nothing to refund.

3/
It covers airtime in 19 African countries, data in 13, and prepaid electricity in Nigeria and Senegal (Woyofal), in English and French.
Lagos in naira, Dakar in CFA.
[video: the NOT CHARGED screen, wallet balance back where it started]

4/
Every completed purchase ends with a receipt signed with Nostr, checked in your browser. No need to trust our server.
