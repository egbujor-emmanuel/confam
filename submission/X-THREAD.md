You buy airtime. Your money is gone. The airtime never comes. Then you chase a refund for weeks. 😩

I built Confam for #DakarBitcoinHack: pay for airtime, data and light with Bitcoin, and if it doesn't arrive, you don't pay.

E no land? You no pay. ⚡

2/
How? Your Bitcoin is held, not sent.
Confam buys the airtime. Only when the network confirms it landed does the payment go through.
If it fails, the hold opens and your money never left your wallet.
