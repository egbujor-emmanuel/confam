# Confam: LinkedIn post

"Debited, but not credited."

If you live in Nigeria, you know that message. You buy airtime or an electricity token, the money leaves your account, and the product never arrives. Then you spend days, sometimes weeks, chasing a refund.

For the Dakar Bitcoin Hack by Btrust, I built Confam to fix that.

Confam lets you pay for airtime, data and prepaid electricity with Bitcoin, with one simple promise: if it doesn't arrive, you don't pay.

How it works, in plain words:
• When you pay, your money is held, not sent.
• Confam buys the airtime or token for you.
• Only when the provider confirms it was delivered does the payment go through.
• If anything fails, the hold opens on its own and your money never leaves your wallet. No refund to chase.

What it covers today:
• Airtime in 19 African countries and data in 13
• Prepaid electricity in Nigeria and in Senegal (Woyofal)
• English and French
